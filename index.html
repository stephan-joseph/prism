Coverage: the base team from the support window and locations

The support window and the delivery locations' working hours set the smallest L2 + L3 team that can staff every hour. No ticket volumes are used. Step 2 builds a second team from the ticket volume. The two are compared at step 4: the larger one is used, so the base team is the floor.

              Transformation capacity

              Stage 3 adds transformation capacity on top of the run team from Stage 2. The framework scenario comes from the client's AI maturity and whether Step 2 demand came from real volumetrics (a tower sized from thin slices does not count). The scenario sets the Transformation POD share; the application life stage and the client's appetite move it, and the clarity of the improvement pathways decides how much of the saving is credited later. The AI engineers (AIOps) for towers with AIOps in scope sit inside the POD. Year 1 shows two lines: the run team and the transformation capacity.
